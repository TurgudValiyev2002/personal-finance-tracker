About this website

This website is a personal finance tracker for recording daily costs, earnings, and savings in a simple way. It helps users see where money goes, compare months, and understand whether savings are improving. The app is used by adding each transaction with a date, amount, category, and short description, then checking the dashboard, statistics, profile summary, and AI Advisor. The result is a clearer view of spending habits and better monthly planning. The AI Advisor adds value by reading the finance data and turning it into practical suggestions, while the local fallback keeps recommendations available even when hosted AI is unavailable. I am Turgud Valiyev, and I created this website to personally track my costs, understand my habits, and increase my savings.
